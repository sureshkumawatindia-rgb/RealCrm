Connect your WhatsApp

Bring your WhatsApp Business number into YELLOW CRM. Your WhatsApp Business app keeps working on your phone, and your chats and contacts come into the CRM's Inbox for your team.

  1. 1Keep WhatsApp Business open on your phone (version 2.24.17 or newer)
  2. 2Click Connect WhatsApp and log in to Facebook in the window that opens
  3. 3Choose connect your existing WhatsApp Business app, enter your number, and scan the QR code that Meta shows with your phone
  4. 4Allow sharing your chat history to bring the last 6 months of chats

Opens Meta's secure window. YELLOW CRM never sees your Facebook password.
