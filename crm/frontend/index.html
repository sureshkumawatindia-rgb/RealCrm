            Questions, answered

            Can I use the WhatsApp number I already have?

            Yes. Connect the number you use in the WhatsApp Business app (version 2.24.17 or newer) and keep using the app on your phone: its contacts and up to 6 months of chats come into the inbox, and what you send from the phone shows there too. A new number works as well. You connect it in Meta's own window, in a few minutes.

            Who pays for WhatsApp messages?

            Meta charges for some messages, mainly templates such as broadcasts, at Meta's own rates, and bills your business directly on your Meta account. agreem360 CRM adds nothing on top: the plan price is for the CRM.

            What happens when the free trial ends?

            Nothing is deleted. Your team can still sign in, read and reply to customers, and new WhatsApp messages and leads keep coming in. Adding more people, contacts, quotations or broadcasts waits until you choose a plan in Settings. Choose during the trial and the rest of the trial stays free.

            Can I change or cancel my plan?

            Yes, any time from Settings. A bigger plan starts at once and a smaller one from the next month. If you cancel, your plan runs until the end of the month you paid for.

            Do I get a GST invoice?

            Yes. Every payment for your plan comes with a GST tax invoice as a PDF, for your accounts.

            Can I choose what each person sees?

            Yes. Everyone signs in with their own Google account, and you choose their role (admin, agent or viewer) and the pages they can open. You can hide customers' phone numbers from agents, and anyone can switch on 2-step verification with a code on WhatsApp.

            Is there a phone app?

            agreem360 CRM works in the phone's browser and can be added to the home screen like an app, with notifications on the phone. Nothing to download from a store.

            Can I take my data out, or delete it?

            Yes. The owner can download all the company's data at any time, and delete the company, in Settings → Data & Privacy. See the Privacy Policy.
