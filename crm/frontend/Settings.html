WhatsApp

Connect your WhatsApp Business number (WhatsApp Cloud API by Meta). Customers' messages then arrive in this CRM: new numbers become contacts and WhatsApp leads automatically.

Message templates

WhatsApp allows only approved templates when a customer has not written in the last 24 hours (and for writing first). Meta reviews every new template, usually within minutes, sometimes up to a day.

New template

Write {{1}}, {{2}} … where you fill in details when sending. Do not start or end the message with one.

Click-to-chat link and QR code

Put the link on your website, IndiaMART profile or email signature, and the QR code on your shop counter, visiting card or packaging. A customer who taps or scans it starts a WhatsApp chat with you, and the chat lands in the Inbox.

Lead sources

Where your enquiries come from. Every enquiry becomes a contact and a lead (the same mobile number is never added twice; a new enquiry from someone with an open lead is added to that lead). Auto-replies and assignment rules follow in the next update.

Connect Facebook Lead Ads

You need a Meta developer app (the WhatsApp one works too), the Page ID, a long-lived Page access token with the permissions leads_retrieval, pages_manage_metadata, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and ads_management, and the app's App secret (App settings → Basic). The CRM checks the token and subscribes the Page to new leads.

Connect IndiaMART

In IndiaMART Seller Panel → Lead Manager → Import/Export Leads → CRM Integration, generate the CRM API key (it comes by email) and paste it here. The CRM then pulls your IndiaMART leads every 5 minutes (IndiaMART allows no more), and IndiaMART can also push each lead at once to the address shown after connecting.

Links and receipts

Inside 24 hours a normal message goes. A template gets the customer's name, the amount, the order number and the link, in that order (or by the names of its variables, e.g. {{customer_name}}, {{amount}}, {{order_number}}, {{link}}); a link button gets the end of the link.

Lead rules

What happens the moment an enquiry arrives (from any lead source, or a customer's first WhatsApp message): who gets the lead, and the instant WhatsApp reply.

Working hours
Who gets a new lead

Rules are tried from the top; the first that fits the enquiry decides. A lead that no rule fits stays unassigned: everyone with "See all" sees it in Leads, and its WhatsApp chat waits in the queue.

Instant WhatsApp reply

Sends one of your approved WhatsApp templates within seconds of a new enquiry, in the chat of the lead's owner, marked "Auto-reply". Repeat enquiries, enquiries older than the limit (for example IndiaMART's first pull of the last 24 hours) and customers without a mobile number get none.

AI assistant

The assistant answers from your company profile, your products and prices (Products) and your FAQ answers (Sales Automation → FAQ bot). It never makes up prices, discounts or delivery dates: when it is not sure, it leaves the chat to your team. To write answers, the customer's recent messages and your product list are sent to Anthropic's Claude API.

Data & Privacy

Storage

Your account, company profile, team, customers, leads, deals, products, quotations, tasks, calendar events, support tickets, notes, documents, campaigns, automations and your team are on the CRM server. The list below shows what is still saved in this browser.

Move my browser data to server

Copies the customers, leads, deals, products, quotations, tasks, calendar events, support tickets, notes, documents, campaigns and automations saved in this browser to the CRM server, and invites your Account Champions, so your whole team sees them. Nothing in this browser is deleted, and running it again skips what was already moved. Open this page on the address where you used the CRM before.

Download CRM data

All of your company's CRM data on the server as one JSON file: customers, leads, quotations, orders, products, WhatsApp chats and messages, templates, lead sources, automations, broadcasts, payments, plan invoices, the audit log and the team. Passwords, keys and tokens are never in it. Uploaded files are not included; download them from Documents or the chat.

Old browser data

Copies the CRM data this browser kept before it moved to the server into a JSON file, or loads such a file into this browser — for example to move it from another address before "Move my browser data to server".

Danger Zone

Deletes the CRM data saved in this browser (older copies of records that are now on the server). Data on the CRM server is not touched. Move your data to the server first. You'll stay signed in.
