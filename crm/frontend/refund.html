Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers what a business (“the company”) pays for its AgreemCRM plan to AgreemCRM (“we”). Payments that a company collects from its own customers through its own payment gateway (Settings → Payments) are between that company and its customers.

Free trial

Every new company gets a free trial of 30 days. Nothing is charged during the trial. If you choose a plan during the trial, Razorpay only sets up the payment authorisation; the first charge happens when the trial ends.

Cancelling

An owner or admin can cancel the plan at any time in Settings → Plan & usage. The plan keeps working until the end of the month (or, for a yearly plan, the year) already paid for, and is not charged again. Nothing is deleted when a plan ends: the company can still sign in, read its data and download it.

Refunds

Plans are billed in advance, monthly or yearly. A month or a year that has started is not refunded, in full or in part, also when you cancel or move to a smaller plan during it.

We refund a payment in full when:

  • you were charged twice for the same month or year;
  • you were charged after you had cancelled;
  • the service did not work for you because of a fault on our side that we could not fix.

Write to us within 30 days of the charge at the address on the Contact page, with the invoice number. An approved refund goes back to the original payment method within 7 working days; your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Changing plans

A bigger plan starts at once. A smaller plan starts with the next month (or year). To move between monthly and yearly payment, cancel the plan and choose the other one when the paid period ends. Each payment comes with a GST tax invoice, which you can download in Settings → Plan & usage.

Failed payments

If a payment fails, Razorpay tries again and the plan shows “Payment due”. If every try fails, the plan stops until a plan is chosen again. Your data stays.

Contact

Questions about a payment: see the Contact page.
