Delivery Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

agreem360 is software that a business uses online, in a browser or as the phone app. It is run by agreem360. Nothing is shipped, and there is no delivery charge.

When you get access

  • Free trial: at once. A company signs up with a Google account and can use the CRM straight away, for 30 days.
  • Paid plan: as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment, usually within a few minutes. The plan shows as active in Settings → Plan & usage, with its GST tax invoice, and the company's owners and admins get a note in the CRM.

Where

The service is sold in India, priced in Indian rupees, and works anywhere with an internet connection.

If something does not arrive

If a paid plan does not show as active within 24 hours of a successful payment, write to us (see the Contact page) with the payment details. We switch the plan on, or refund the payment as described in the Cancellation & Refund Policy.
