What is an Agent?

An agent is a team member you add to this CRM. You can assign them a role that matches their day-to-day responsibility, grant them access to only the CRM modules they need, and control exactly what they're allowed to view, create, edit, or delete inside those modules. Modules you don't select here won't appear in their sidebar after they log in.

  • Assign a role or responsibility
  • Grant access to specific CRM modules only
  • Set granular View / Create / Edit / Delete permissions
