YELLOW CRM

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

YELLOW CRM is a customer relationship management service for businesses, run by YELLOW CRM (“we”). This policy explains what we collect when a business and its team use YELLOW CRM, why, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible for what

A business that signs up (“the company”) decides which customers, chats and documents it keeps in YELLOW CRM; for that data the company is responsible and we process it on its behalf. For the accounts of the people who log in, and for running the service, we are responsible.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and profile photo from Google when you sign in, and — if you switch on 2-step verification — your mobile number.
  • The company's records: customers, leads, quotations, orders, payments, tasks, documents and notes that the team enters or imports.
  • WhatsApp: when the company connects its WhatsApp Business number through Meta, the messages it sends and receives, the names and numbers of the people it chats with and, if the company allows it, its WhatsApp Business app contacts and chat history of up to the last six months. Group chats are not received.
  • Usage and security data: the browsers and devices you log in from, an audit log of important actions, and technical logs needed to keep the service working and safe.

How we use it

Only to provide YELLOW CRM to the company: to show its inbox and records to its team, send the messages its team writes, run the automations the company sets up, keep accounts secure, bill the company for its plan, and support it when asked. We do not sell personal data and do not use WhatsApp messages for advertising.

Services we use

We share data only as needed to run the service: Google (sign-in), Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform, when the company connects WhatsApp), our hosting and database providers, and — only when the company switches them on — Razorpay or Cashfree (payments), MSG91 (login codes by SMS) and Anthropic (the optional AI assistant). Each uses the data only to provide its part of the service.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

The company's data stays as long as the company uses YELLOW CRM. An owner can download all of it at any time and can delete the company (both in Settings → Data & Privacy): after a 7-day grace period, all its customers, chats, files and records are erased. Plan invoices are kept as tax law requires.

Data deletion requests. To have your own account or data received through WhatsApp deleted, ask the company you work with, or write to us at the email address on your invoice with your name, your email or phone number and the company's name. We confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Access tokens and keys of connected services are stored encrypted. Every person has their own login, and owners decide what each teammate can see. You can see and log out every browser where your account is open (Settings → Your Profile).

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your personal data, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw consent you gave, subject to the law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). Contact us as below.
